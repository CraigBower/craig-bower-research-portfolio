# Craig Bower — Research Portfolio

Research in **scientific machine learning, uncertainty quantification, sequential decision-making and autonomous scientific systems**.

This repository contains the source for my research portfolio, built with [Quarto](https://quarto.org/). The portfolio presents selected research projects with an emphasis on the mathematical ideas behind them, the scientific problems they address, how the methods are evaluated, and the limits of the evidence.

## Research focus

My work asks how intelligent systems can learn, predict and make decisions when information is incomplete, expensive to acquire, uncertain or changing through time.

Across different applications, this leads to a common set of questions:

- How should scientific knowledge be encoded into machine-learning models?
- How can uncertainty remain meaningful when models move beyond their training regime?
- How should a system decide what to observe, simulate or measure next?
- How can learned systems adapt online without losing the structure learned previously?
- When can we prove that architectural structure or adaptive decision-making improves generalisation or efficiency?

## Selected projects

### HyperFlux

A self-learning scientific digital twin for carbon exchange in intensively managed lowland peat agriculture.

HyperFlux combines explicit ecosystem physics, graph-based learning, uncertainty-aware prediction and sequential local adaptation. It supports forecasting, counterfactual analysis, climate-scenario projection and prediction at locations without flux towers.

[Read the HyperFlux case study](https://craigbower.github.io/craig-bower-research-portfolio/projects/hyperflux/)

---

### Physics-Enforced Continuous Neural Operator (PeCNO)

A continuous neural-field surrogate for astrophysical jet simulations.

PeCNO combines continuous space-time prediction, hard physical projection, conformal uncertainty calibration, online adaptive recalibration and controlled generative recovery of smaller spatial scales.

[Read the PeCNO case study](https://craigbower.github.io/craig-bower-research-portfolio/projects/pecno/)

---

### Variable-Interaction Graph Networks (VIGNet)

A graph neural-operator framework whose connectivity is derived from the interactions that actually exist in the governing equations.

The work studies universal approximation, the relationship between graph distance and required network depth, generalisation under dependent data, sample complexity and the consequences of graph mis-specification.

[Read the VIGNet case study](https://craigbower.github.io/craig-bower-research-portfolio/projects/vignet/)

---

### ABBDA — Learning Where to Look

Adaptive sensing for quickest change detection when observing every stream is prohibitively expensive.

ABBDA learns how to allocate a hard observation budget across large monitoring systems while preserving exploration, detecting persistent changes and identifying their source.

[Read the ABBDA case study](https://craigbower.github.io/craig-bower-research-portfolio/projects/abbda/)

---

### Adaptive Scientific Campaigns

A developing research programme concerned with the value of scientific computation and the design of self-steering simulation campaigns.

The central question is how expensive simulations should be selected so that each additional computation delivers defensible scientific knowledge rather than merely reducing model uncertainty.
